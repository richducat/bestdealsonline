What to expect under $15

At this price, most air fryer listings are entry-level: fewer premium materials, simpler features, and a shorter (or no) extended warranty. That's fine if you want a low-risk first buy, a replacement for something that broke, or a gift the recipient may not use constantly. Skim the star-rating breakdown rather than just the average -- a 4.3 built on thousands of reviews is safer than a 4.6 built on twelve. Watch for zero-review, unbranded sellers; a slightly higher price from an established brand is often worth it at this tier.
